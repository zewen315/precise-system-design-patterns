## Preparation Roadmap

1. Understand the interview process and candidate expectations.
2. Build intuition from example interviews (recordings). Maintain a learning TODO list.
3. Learn the fundamental concepts, technologies, and how they fit into a system design interview.
4. Master the building blocks.
5. Learn common design patterns by solving classic system design problems, and continuously refine your knowledge.
6. Practice under interview conditions using a wide range of case studies.
7. Gain experience through real interviews.
